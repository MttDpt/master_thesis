# Cited papers and annotations

[Download cited-papers.zip](cited-papers.zip): 45 scholarly PDFs and all 453 active stored Zotero annotations, with exact JSON sidecars, comments, positions and colours. The [manifest](manifest.json) maps the final thesis's 48 citation keys to archived files; [cited bibliography](cited-references.bib), PDF checks inside the ZIP and [ZIP integrity](archive-check.json) are retained.

Thirty-nine PDFs came from local Zotero attachments; six missing local copies were recovered from publisher/university/author sources. Three citations are web resources with URL shortcuts and available HTML snapshots. The Google Crazy 8s URL now serves a different landing page; its original URL is retained without substituting content. The Coming Age of Calm Technology is the explicitly labelled 1996 manuscript corresponding to the cited 1997 book chapter.

No literature notes, wiki or syntheses are included.
