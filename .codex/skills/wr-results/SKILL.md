# Results Writing

Load `wr-general` and the relevant approved results; read contract clauses only to resolve a measure or interpretation boundary. Sources: Mensh–Kording rule 7; Wobbrock's Results; CHI result statements adapted to the evidence.

- Open with the question and a brief reminder of the approach, enough for readers who skipped Methods.
- Follow RQ order; within each RQ, arrange findings as logical steps supporting the answer. Quantitative subsections may follow measures; qualitative subsections may follow supported themes. Lead quantitative passages with comparisons and relevant cross-insights, then supply the supporting values, rather than listing each condition's row; avoid repeating findings solely to introduce another measure.
- Build each passage as question/context → data and reasoning → bounded answer. Use supported declarative findings as headings or topic sentences when useful, without inflating certainty.
- State analysed populations, exclusions, denominators, measures, and units before rates or comparisons. Report direction, magnitude, relevant uncertainty, and important exceptions.
- If approved statistical tests exist, explain what the result means in the measured outcomes, alongside the statistics; a p-value or “significant effect” alone is insufficient. Do not introduce tests or causal conclusions.
- Let figures and tables carry detailed values; prose explains the pattern. A figure title or caption should identify its supported message, and the legend should explain the measure, sample, method, and uncertainty needed to read it independently.
- Report null, anomalous, and inconclusive findings when they affect the answer. For qualitative findings, substantiate themes through coding evidence and illustrative quotations; claim prevalence only with a defensible denominator.
- Keep Q1/Q2 outcomes and ordinal confidence distinct. For RQ1, report detection before latency conditional on detection; gaze remains contextual evidence.
- Report observations dispassionately; mechanisms, broader implications, recommendations, and speculation belong in Discussion. Neither “clearly shows” nor a smooth narrative licenses claims of comprehension, safety, effectiveness, or generalisability.
- Close with the supported answer only when it adds information; avoid repeated summaries.

Preserve approved values, citations, labels, and interpretation boundaries. Prose drafting does not require rerunning analysis or regenerating reports.
