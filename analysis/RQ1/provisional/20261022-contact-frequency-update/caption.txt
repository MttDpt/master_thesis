Figure 4.5 — contacts per minute of the full analysis window. Recorded ambient-display contact frequency in missed and detected animated trials, pooled and by animation. Each point is one usable trial-window; the y-axis is recorded contacts divided by scheduled window length in minutes, and the x-axis separates 30–90 s and 90–120 s fixed periods. Colour and shape identify the animation; hollow markers denote misses and filled markers detections. Box lines show medians, boxes the middle 50%, and whiskers extend to the most extreme points within 1.5 interquartile ranges; all observations are shown. Labels give usable/eligible counts: 66/96 usable animated windows in total (35/48 onset; 31/48 completion), retaining the existing coverage rule. Sun-to-moon has no onset misses. The pooled group repeats the individual-animation observations, and participants contribute repeated trials. These are descriptive distributions, not confidence intervals or significance tests; unrecorded contacts cannot be recovered, and recorded segments may reflect fragmentation rather than intentional looks.
