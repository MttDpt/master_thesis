# Retained final analysis

Start with the [Chapter 4 inferential authority](inference_ch4.md), [preparation rules](rules_preprocessing.md) and [evidence map](index.md).

Definitions are preserved in the [RQ1](contract_RQ1.md), [RQ2](contract_RQ2.md) and [RQ3](contract_RQ3.md) contracts; historical or proposed questions within those frozen records are superseded by the inferential authority. [Limitations](limitations.md) remain part of interpretation.

The retained `dataset/` contains the included 72-trial source/derived records, source questionnaire workbooks and formative workshop data. `RQ1/`, `RQ2/` and `RQ3/` contain only selected final outputs/generators and the predecessor files they actually read or hash. The [final supplementary report](additional-measures/additional-measures-final.html) uses 24 eligible UEQ participants and 72 NASA-TLX trial responses.
