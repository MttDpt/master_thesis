# Access and redistribution

This repository is private by design.

The evidence contains pseudonymised participant-level gaze, driving and questionnaire data, verbatim trial transcripts and demographic/context information from a small employee sample. Pseudonyms do not establish anonymisation: combinations of attributes or transcript details may identify people. The retained aggregate questionnaire source workbook also preserves excluded-session rows needed to audit original exclusions; unused final interviews and excluded trial transcripts are omitted. A check of the aggregate workbook found no email cells or direct-identifier column headers, which is not a disclosure review of every free-text value.

The cited-source ZIP contains third-party publications and personal Zotero annotations. Original copyright and licence terms remain with the rights holders; public redistribution permission has not been established. Publicly downloadable copies are not assumed to be openly licensed.

Do not change visibility or distribute this complete archive publicly without reviewing participant consent, organisational confidentiality, publication rights and the contents of annotations/transcripts. No blanket licence is applied to third-party papers, template materials or study data. The final thesis PDF includes workshop artefacts and simulator images and also needs review before public redistribution.
